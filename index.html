About the institute

Knowledge with a wider perspective

Supragnopsyche Institute for Integral Consciousness, Vedanta & Psychological Research & Publication (SIICVPRP) is an educational and intellectual initiative established in 2020. Its interests include education, psychological understanding, research, philosophy, culture, creativity and the holistic development of individuals.

We aim to create accessible opportunities for learning and reflection through educational programmes, cultural initiatives, study resources and research-oriented activities.

01

Mission

Encourage accessible education, thoughtful inquiry, creativity and human development.

02

Vision

A learning community where knowledge, scientific curiosity and cultural understanding grow together.

03

Approach

Interdisciplinary learning, respect for diverse ideas and practical skill-building.
